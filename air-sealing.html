What is air sealing in the woodland, Texas?

Air sealing in the woodland, Texas is the process of sealing gaps and cracks in your home's envelope to prevent air leaks. This service involves using materials like spray foam and caulk to fill openings around windows, doors, and other structural elements, ensuring your home remains energy-efficient.

Local homeowners in the woodland often experience issues such as drafty rooms and high energy bills due to inadequate air sealing. The air sealing process explained

01

Initial assessment

Our team conducts a thorough inspection of your home using thermal imaging cameras to identify air leaks. This step typically takes 1-2 hours, ensuring we pinpoint all areas needing attention.

02

Preparation

Before sealing, we prepare the area by cleaning and removing any obstacles. This ensures a proper seal and may take an additional hour, depending on the size of the space.

03

Sealing gaps

Using high-quality materials like spray foam and caulk, we seal identified gaps and cracks. This process can take several hours, depending on the extent of the work required.

04

Final inspection

After sealing, we perform a final inspection to ensure all areas are properly sealed. This step includes a follow-up thermal imaging scan to verify effectiveness.

05

Customer walkthrough

Once the job is complete, we walk you through the work done, explaining the improvements made and how to maintain your newly sealed home.
