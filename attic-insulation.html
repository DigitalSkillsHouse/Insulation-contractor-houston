How North Houston Heat and Humidity Affect Your Attic Insulation

This is what's happening in your attic right now that no one else is talking about.

Attic Temperatures Hit 140°F in July

North Houston attics regularly reach 130–145°F during summer. That heat radiates down through the attic floor into your living space. Proper blown-in insulation at R-38 or higher puts a real barrier between that heat and your home — thin or compressed insulation doesn't.

Humidity Degrades Blown-In Insulation Over 10–15 Years

Spring TX averages 75% relative humidity from spring through fall. Over time, that moisture finds its way into your attic through air gaps and penetrations. Blown-in fiberglass absorbs moisture, compresses, and loses R-value. A 15-year-old insulation job in a North Houston attic is often performing at half its original rating.

Poor Insulation Shortens Your AC System's Life

When your attic is under-insulated, your AC runs longer cycles to fight the constant heat load coming through the ceiling. A compressor that should last 15 years gets pushed to failure in 10. Proper attic insulation is one of the best things you can do for your HVAC system — and it's a fraction of what an AC replacement costs.

Mold Prevention Starts in the Attic

Humid air entering through attic gaps brings moisture into your insulation and eventually into your ceilings and walls. Air sealing at the attic level — before new insulation goes in — breaks that cycle. We find and seal those entry points during every inspection as a standard part of the job.
