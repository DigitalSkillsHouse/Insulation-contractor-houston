Attic Insulation Options for Houston Homes

We install two primary products in attics across North Houston, each suited to different attic types and goals.

Blown-In Fiberglass Insulation

Loose-fill fiberglass blown to uniform depth across your attic floor. Ideal for attics with existing insulation being topped off to current R-value standards. Cost: $1.00 to $2.50 per square foot installed.

Open-Cell Spray Foam (Attic Encapsulation)

Sprayed directly onto the underside of roof decking to create an unvented conditioned attic space. Seals the entire roof deck and eliminates attic air infiltration. Cost: $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot installed.

Old Insulation Removal

Full vacuum removal of existing blown-in or batt insulation before a new installation. Recommended when existing material is contaminated, moisture-damaged, or pest-affected. Cost: $1.00 to $2.00 per square foot.

How Our Attic Insulation Process Works

Most residential attic insulation jobs are completed in a single day.

1

Free In-Home Inspection

We inspect your attic, measure existing insulation depth, and check for moisture, pest damage, or air sealing gaps. You receive a written estimate before any work begins.

2

Old Material Removal (if needed)

If existing insulation is damaged, contaminated, or pest-affected, we vacuum it out completely before installation. Bags are sealed and removed from the property.

3

Air Sealing

We seal penetrations around electrical wires, plumbing, recessed lights, and top plates with foam or caulk before any insulation is installed. Air sealing amplifies the R-value of new material.

4

Insulation Installation

We install blown-in fiberglass or open-cell spray foam to the target R-value, checking depth at multiple points across the attic to ensure even coverage.

5

Final Check & Clean-Up

We verify installed depth, clear all work areas, and walk you through the completed job. The house is clean before we leave.

Attic Insulation Questions — Houston TX

How much does attic insulation cost in Houston TX?

Blown-in attic insulation in Houston typically runs $1.00 to $2.50 per square foot installed. Open-cell spray foam runs $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. Most residential jobs — 1,500 to 2,500 square feet of attic — come in between $1,200 and $4,500 depending on the product and whether removal is included. Contact us for a free itemized estimate.

What R-value does my Houston attic need?

The DOE recommends R-38 to R-60 for Houston (Climate Zone 2A). The prescriptive minimum under the 2021 IECC is R-49. Most North Houston homes built before 2000 have R-11 to R-19 and benefit significantly from an upgrade to R-38 or higher.

Do I need to remove old insulation before adding new?

Not necessarily. If existing insulation is dry and undamaged, new blown-in material can be added directly on top. Removal is recommended when the existing material is moisture-damaged, contaminated by pests, or otherwise compromised. We assess this during the free inspection.

How long does attic insulation installation take?

A blown-in attic insulation job for a 1,500 to 2,500 square foot home takes 2 to 4 hours. If removal is included, add 3 to 6 hours. Most jobs are completed in a single day. We give you a specific time window at the time of booking.

What areas do you serve for attic insulation?

We serve all of North Houston including Cypress, Spring, The Woodlands, Conroe, Kingwood, Humble, and Tomball. Free estimates available Monday through Saturday, 8am to 6pm. Call (832) 291-2738 or submit the form above.

Will new attic insulation lower my energy bills?

Upgrading from R-19 to R-38 or R-49 typically reduces heat gain through the ceiling by 40 to 60 percent. Most Houston homeowners see 15 to 30 percent reductions in summer cooling costs. Results depend on your home's age, air sealing condition, and HVAC system efficiency.
