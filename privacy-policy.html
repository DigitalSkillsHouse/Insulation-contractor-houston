Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025  |  Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Who We Are

Insulation Contractor Houston ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website insulationcontractorhouston.com. We are a licensed insulation contractor serving Greater Houston, TX. Our address is Cypress Farms Ranch Rd, Houston, TX 77429. You can reach us by phone at (832) 291-2738 or by email at info@insulationcontractorhouston.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Submit a contact or quote request form (name, phone, email, ZIP code, service description)
  • Call us directly (caller ID and call content)
  • Click a phone number link on our website (we log the page and time of the click)

We also collect certain information automatically through analytics tools (see Section 4):

  • Pages visited, time on page, referring URL
  • Browser type, device type, approximate location (city-level)
  • IP address (anonymized where possible)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request or inquiry
  • To schedule and perform insulation services at your property
  • To follow up on estimates and completed work
  • To improve our website and service quality
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Analytics & Cookies

Our website uses Google Analytics (Google Tag Manager, GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data including pages visited and session duration. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not use advertising cookies or retargeting pixels.

5. Data Retention

Lead and contact form submissions are stored in our secure database and retained for up to 3 years for business records purposes. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of future communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@insulationcontractorhouston.com or call (832) 291-2738.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), server-side authentication, and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (Google Maps, YouTube, Pinterest). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies directly.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
