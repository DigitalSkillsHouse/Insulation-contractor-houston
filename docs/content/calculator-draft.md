## What Affects Insulation Cost in Houston?

Attic size comes first, then whether old insulation has to come out, then the type of insulation. Seven things move your price:

- **Attic size.** More square feet means more material and more crew time.
- **Current insulation.** A thin or damaged layer changes how much goes in and where your price lands in the range.
- **Target R-value.** Reaching R-49 takes more material than R-38.
- **Insulation type.** Blown in fiberglass costs $1.00 to $2.50 per square foot. Spray foam costs $2.00 to $4.50 and also seals air leaks.
- **Attic access.** Low roof lines, small hatches, and stored items add labor time.
- **Removal.** Wet, moldy, or pest soiled insulation has to come out first, at $1.00 to $1.50 per square foot.
- **Air sealing.** Sealing gaps around pipes, wires, and the attic hatch costs $250 to $740 per attic.

## How Much Insulation Do I Need for My Attic?

You need about 17 to 22 inches of blown in fiberglass if your attic has little or no insulation. If it already has 3 to 4 inches, add about 9 to 14 more. Those depths reach R-49 and R-38, and we measure your attic in several spots to confirm.

## What R-Value Do I Need for My Attic in Houston?

Aim for R-49 if your Houston attic has little or no insulation, and R-38 if it already has 3 to 4 inches. ENERGY STAR recommends both for our climate zone. R-value measures how well insulation resists heat flow, so a higher number keeps more heat out of your rooms.
