### H2: How Much Insulation Do I Need for My Attic?

You need about 17 to 22 inches of blown in fiberglass if your attic has little or no insulation. If it already has 3 to 4 inches, add about 9 to 14 more. Those depths reach R-49 and R-38, and we measure your attic in several spots to confirm.

Found in: Quora: how thick should blown attic insulation be; how to calculate how much insulation I need; autocomplete

### H2: How Long Does It Take for Attic Insulation to Pay for Itself?

In bills alone, often 10 years or more. ENERGY STAR estimates air sealing plus insulation saves about 6% of a Houston energy bill. On a $200 monthly bill that is about $12 a month, so a $1,500 job takes about 10 years to pay back. The bigger gain is a cooler upstairs.

Found in: Reddit: payback and ROI posts, 'is it worth it' posts in 2026; autocomplete: attic insulation savings

### H2: Why Didn't My Energy Bill Drop After Adding Insulation?

Usually because air leaks, not thin insulation, are still doing the damage. Insulation slows heat but does not stop air, so open gaps around can lights, pipes, and the attic hatch keep leaking. We seal first and insulate second, so the walkthrough checks leaks before depth.

Found in: Reddit: 'spent $2,400 adding insulation and my bills barely moved' (air sealing)

### H2: Do I Need to Remove Old Insulation Before Adding New?

Not always. If the old insulation is dry and clean, new insulation can go on top to reach your target depth. It has to come out if it is wet, moldy, or soiled by pests. We check this on the walkthrough and list removal as its own line on your quote.

Found in: Reddit: 'is removing old insulation necessary' and add on top vs remove threads

### H2: Is Spray Foam Worth the Extra Cost Over Blown In?

Sometimes. Spray foam costs $2.00 to $4.50 per square foot, compared with $1.00 to $2.50 for blown in, but it seals air leaks and can go on the roof deck. Blown in fiberglass is the better value on most attic floors. Spray foam fits when you want the roof deck sealed.

Found in: Reddit: 'is spray foam really worth the extra cost' (Aug 2026); Quora: is spray foam more expensive than fiberglass

### H2: Do Insulation Companies Charge for an Estimate?

Some do, but we do not. Our attic walkthrough and written quote are free, with no conditions. You can get a free insulation estimate in Houston and still shop around. Ask any company whether its estimate is free before you book, and what the quote will list.

Found in: Reddit r/houston: contractors who do not charge a service fee for a quote

### H2: Why Are Insulation Quotes So Different From Each Other?

Quotes differ because they cover different work. One may include removal, air sealing, and baffles, and another may not. Depth, target R-value, and material also change the price. Ask each company to list the same items in writing, then compare the total and the price per square foot.

Found in: Reddit: 'every quote is different' complaints in several threads
