How Much Does Attic Insulation Cost in Houston in 2026?

A typical 1,500 square foot Houston attic costs about $1,500 to $3,750 with blown in insulation and $3,000 to $6,750 with spray foam. Blown in costs less per square foot, while spray foam also seals air leaks and covers the roof deck, which is a larger surface than the attic floor.

What Is the Insulation Cost per Square Foot in Houston, TX?

In Houston, TX, insulation costs $1.00 to $2.50 per square foot for blown in and $2.00 to $4.50 for spray foam. Injection foam for walls runs $2.00 to $4.20 per square foot of wall. Taking old insulation out adds $1.00 to $1.50 per square foot, and full replacement runs $2.00 to $4.00.

What R-Value Do I Need for My Attic in Houston?

Aim for R-49 if your Houston attic has little or no insulation, and R-38 if it already has 3 to 4 inches. ENERGY STAR recommends both for our climate zone. R-value measures how well insulation resists heat flow, so a higher number keeps more heat out of your rooms.

What Affects Insulation Cost in Houston?

Attic size has the biggest effect on what you pay, because every square foot adds material and crew time. Removing old insulation comes next, then the type you choose, blown in or spray foam. Access, your R-value target, and air sealing round out the seven factors below.

Attic size

More square feet means more material and more crew time. At 1,500 square feet, blown in insulation costs about $1,500 to $3,750.

Current insulation

A thin or damaged layer changes how much goes in and where your price lands in the range. We measure depth in several spots to see what you have.

Target R-value

Reaching R-49 takes more material than R-38. In blown in fiberglass, that is about 17 to 22 inches for R-49 and 13 to 17 inches for R-38.

Insulation type

Blown in fiberglass costs $1.00 to $2.50 per square foot. Spray foam costs $2.00 to $4.50 and also seals air leaks. Blown in goes on the attic floor, and spray foam can go on the roof deck.

Attic access

Low roof lines, small hatches, and stored items add labor time. A clear path to the hatch helps the crew work faster.

Removal

Wet, moldy, or pest soiled insulation has to come out first, at $1.00 to $1.50 per square foot. Replacing it with new blown in insulation runs $2.00 to $4.00 per square foot.

Air sealing

Sealing gaps around pipes, wires, and the attic hatch costs $250 to $740 per attic. We seal first and insulate second, because insulation over open gaps loses much of its value.

How Does R-Value Affect Insulation Cost?

A higher R-value costs more because it takes more material to reach. In Houston, R-49 takes about 17 to 22 inches of blown in fiberglass and R-38 takes about 13 to 17, so R-49 sits higher in the price range. R-value measures how well insulation resists heat flow, and higher is better.
