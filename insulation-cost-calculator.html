What Affects Your Insulation Cost in Houston?

📐

Square Footage

The largest single factor. Larger attics cost more in materials and labor time — but cost per sq ft often drops for bigger jobs.

🌡️

Current vs. Target R-Value

If you already have R-11 insulation, you're adding less material to reach R-38 than a home with zero insulation. That gap directly sets material volume.

🔧

Insulation Type

Blown-in is the lowest-cost option. Open-cell spray foam costs roughly 1.5–2× more per sq ft. Closed-cell is premium but provides the highest R-value per inch.

🏚️

Attic Access & Conditions

Low-pitch rooflines, small hatches, knee walls, or stored items all add labor time. Moisture damage or pest contamination may require remediation first.

🗑️

Removal Needed?

Old batts or contaminated loose-fill insulation must be removed before spray foam. Removal typically adds $1–$2/sq ft to the total project cost.

💨

Air Sealing

Spray foam naturally air-seals; blown-in does not. Adding air sealing (gaps around penetrations, top plates) improves performance and typically adds $250–$1,500 to a project.
