Commercial Building Types We Insulate

  • Metal buildings & steel structures — closed-cell spray foam applied directly to metal panels eliminates condensation, adds structural rigidity, and delivers R-12 to R-19 in 2–3 inches.
  • Warehouses & distribution centers — roof deck spray foam dramatically reduces cooling loads. Uninsulated metal roofs in North Houston reach 160°F in summer.
  • Office buildings — attic blown-in upgrades, exterior wall injection foam, and air sealing to reduce energy costs and improve indoor comfort.
  • Retail & light commercial — service bays, strip mall units, and mixed-use spaces. We work around your schedule to minimize business disruption.
  • Agricultural & equestrian — barn and stable insulation to protect livestock and equipment from Houston's summer heat extremes.

Commercial Insulation Systems

  • Closed-cell spray foam — the standard for metal buildings and roof decks. Seamless air and vapor barrier, highest R-value per inch (R-6 to R-7), structurally reinforcing.
  • Open-cell spray foam — cost-effective for interior commercial attic encapsulation and sound control in office partitions.
  • Blown-in fiberglass — fast installation for large commercial attic floor areas. R-38 or R-49 to reduce HVAC load in office and retail spaces.
  • Injection foam — retrofit wall insulation for existing commercial buildings without exterior cladding removal.
  • Air sealing — commercial buildings leak far more air than residential. We identify and seal bypasses at top plates, penetrations, and mechanical chases.
