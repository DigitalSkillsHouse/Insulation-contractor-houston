The Spray Foam Insulation Process

01

Initial Consultation

A specialist assesses your home to identify insulation needs. This step typically takes about 30 minutes and helps determine the best approach.

02

Preparation

The area is prepared by sealing off spaces and covering furniture. This ensures that the application process is clean and efficient.

03

Application

Using a spray foam gun, the insulation is applied to the designated areas. This process usually takes 2-4 hours depending on the size of the project.

04

Curing Time

The foam requires a curing time of 24 hours to expand fully and set. During this period, it's essential to keep the area ventilated.

05

Final Inspection

A thorough inspection ensures that the spray foam insulation has been applied correctly and meets all energy standards. This step confirms the effectiveness of the installation.
