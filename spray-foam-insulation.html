Attic Encapsulation and Air Sealing for Cypress Homes

Bringing your attic into your home's thermal envelope changes how your air conditioner operates during Texas heat waves.

✓

Unconditioned vs Conditioned Attic

By spraying open-cell foam under roof rafters, your attic stays within 5 to 10 degrees of your living room. Your AC unit no longer operates in a 140 degree oven.

✓

Humidity & Duct Moisture Control

Gulf Coast humidity can cause ductwork to sweat in hot attics. Spray foam seals out outdoor moisture, protecting ceiling drywall from water spots and mold growth.

✓

Open-Cell vs Closed-Cell Physics

Open-cell foam (R-3.7 per inch) stays flexible and vapor-permeable, making leak detection simple. Closed-cell foam (R-6.5 per inch) adds rigid strength to metal buildings.

✓

Building Code Compliance

All installations meet International Residential Code (IRC 2021) guidelines for Climate Zone 2A, using low-VOC blowing agents approved under the EPA SNAP program.

Our 5 Step Spray Foam Installation Process

From initial thermal scan to final cleanup, our certified technicians treat your home with complete care.

01

In-Home Inspection

We inspect your attic decking, measure rafter depth, and scan hot spots using thermal imaging cameras to calculate precise square footage.

02

Site Protection

We lay drop cloths along hallways and cover HVAC equipment, water heaters, and electrical runs with protective plastic sheeting.

03

Precision Application

Using a calibrated proportioner rig, our crew sprays open-cell or closed-cell foam in uniform layers to hit exact R-value targets (see our Houston Attic R-Value Guide for local DOE standards).

04

Negative Air Ventilation
