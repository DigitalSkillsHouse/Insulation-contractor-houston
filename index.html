Why Houston Homes Need Better Attic Insulation

Attic insulation directly affects how much you pay to cool your home in Houston. Most Houston TX homes built in the 1980s and 1990s along the US-290 and SH-249 corridors were installed with R-11 to R-19 insulation, and today's minimum for Harris County is R-38. That gap means your AC is working harder than it needs to every summer. Hurricanes Ike and Harvey made it worse in many homes — insulation that absorbed moisture loses R-value and can hold mold for years without any visible sign.

Signs Your Houston Attic Needs Attention

Upstairs rooms that stay hot even when the AC is running, AC units cycling on and off more than usual through July and August, dust coming through vents, rodent droppings, or a musty smell when you open the attic hatch. Any one of these is worth a free walkthrough. Most Houston TX homeowners who call us find their attic insulation is either well below R-20, moisture-damaged, or both.

How do you know when attic insulation needs replacing in Houston TX?

Check for bare or thin insulation in the attic, hot rooms on the top floor, high cooling bills compared to neighbors in similar homes, or any history of roof leaks or flooding. If the home was built in the 1980s or 1990s and the insulation has never been replaced, it likely needs upgrading.

What R-Value Does a Houston Attic Need?

Most Houston TX attics need R-38 at minimum. Harris County falls in IECC Climate Zone 2A, and the U.S. Department of Energy recommends R-38 to R-60 for attic floors in this zone. Most homes built in the 1980s or 1990s measure R-11 to R-19 after decades of settling. Some homeowners go to R-49 on homes with large south or west-facing roof sections where the heat load is highest.

How much attic insulation does a Houston TX home need?

R-38 is the current minimum for Harris County per IECC Climate Zone 2A. If your Houston attic measures below R-20, upgrading to R-38 will reduce the heat load on your AC and lower monthly cooling costs. R-49 is worth considering for homes with large south or west-facing roof areas.

Attic Insulation FAQs — Houston TX Homeowners

These are the questions Houston TX homeowners ask most before scheduling an inspection. If yours is not here, ask us directly and we will answer it before you book.

Blown-in insulation lasts 20 to 30 years under normal conditions. In Houston TX, humidity and heat accelerate settling. Homes built in the 1980s or 1990s with original insulation are almost always below R-20 by now and due for an upgrade.

Insulation slows heat transfer but does not control humidity on its own. Air sealing at the penetration points reduces the amount of humid outside air entering the attic. Together, insulation plus air sealing reduce both heat load and moisture infiltration.

Most Houston TX attic jobs take half a day. A 1,500 sq ft attic with a straight blown-in top-off is typically done in 3 to 4 hours. Jobs that include removal or spray foam at the penetration points take longer.

You do not need to leave. We work in the attic with the hatch open. There is dust involved during blown-in installation, so if anyone in the home has respiratory sensitivities, staying out during the job is a reasonable precaution.

Most Houston TX homeowners save $80–$150 per month on cooling bills after upgrading attic insulation to R-38. The exact savings depend on current R-value, home size, and AC runtime. A home going from R-11 to R-38 typically sees the difference within the first billing cycle after the upgrade.

Blown-in fiberglass is the best attic insulation for most Houston TX homes. It installs quickly, fills around obstructions, and brings most attics from under R-20 to R-38 in one visit. Closed-cell spray foam is the better choice for roof decks and areas where moisture resistance matters. The right answer depends on your attic type, current R-value, and whether air sealing is also needed.
