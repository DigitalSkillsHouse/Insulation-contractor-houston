Why Houston Homes Need Better Attic Insulation

Houston homes need better attic insulation because the roof bakes in the sun for months, and thin insulation lets that heat into your rooms. Houston averages about 111 days a year at 90°F or higher. More insulation keeps upstairs rooms cooler, and with air sealing it cuts heating and cooling costs by about 9%.

What That Means for Your Home

  • A long cooling season. Your AC fights hot weather from late spring into fall, so a weak attic costs you for many months.
  • Old insulation wears out. Over the years it flattens, shifts, or gets wet, and a thin layer slows far less heat than it did when it was new.
  • Ducts in the attic. If your AC ducts run through the attic, they sit in the hottest air in the house, and thicker insulation helps protect them.

Why Older Homes Around 77080 Need a Closer Look

Many homes in Spring Branch were built in the 1950s and 1960s, and many of the western neighborhoods are brick ranch style houses. Insulation put in decades ago may be thin, flattened, or moved around by past repairs. The only way to know is to measure it.

Signs Your Attic Needs More Insulation

  • Upstairs rooms feel hotter than downstairs rooms
  • The AC runs long after the sun goes down
  • Electric bills climb with no other cause
  • You can see the tops of the ceiling joists
  • The insulation looks flat, wet, dirty, or uneven
  • Dust or a musty smell comes through the attic hatch

Is Spray Foam or Blown-In Better for a Houston Attic?

Blown-in fiberglass costs less and works well on the attic floor. Spray foam costs more, seals air leaks, and can go on the roof deck. Pick based on your budget, whether your ducts run through the attic, and how long you plan to stay. We price both for your attic so you can compare.

Is Adding Attic Insulation Worth It in Houston?

Yes, when your attic has thin or damaged insulation, or your upstairs rooms stay hot. The gain is often more comfort than cash, because average savings are modest.

It pays off most when:

  1. Your upstairs is hard to cool
  2. Your insulation is thin, flat, or damaged
  3. You plan to stay in the home for several years
  4. You want the house to hold its temperature longer during an outage

That last point matters here. After Hurricane Beryl in July 2024, about 2.26 million CenterPoint customers lost power, and most were back within about 10 days. Insulation slows how fast a house heats up. It cannot replace power.

Where Do Houston Homes Leak Air?

Air leaks let cool air out and hot attic air in. Insulation slows heat, but it does not stop air, so we seal first and insulate second. Air sealing and insulation work best together.

The usual leak spots in a Houston attic are:

  • Recessed can lights
  • Gaps around wires and plumbing pipes
  • The attic hatch or pull down stairs
  • Duct boots and bath fan housings
  • The tops of interior walls

We seal these with foam or caulk before any blown-in fiberglass goes down. Insulation that sits on top of open gaps loses much of its value.
