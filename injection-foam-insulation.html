Understanding Injection Foam Insulation in Cypress, TX

Injection foam insulation is a specialized wall retrofit service designed for existing homes. Unlike spray foam applied to open attic framing, injection foam is a low-expansion aminoplast resin injected directly into closed exterior wall cavities from the outside.

Homes built across Cypress in neighborhoods like Fairfield, Bridgeland, Towne Lake, and Coles Crossing often suffer from empty or poorly insulated exterior wall studs. During hot Texas summers along US-290 and Grand Parkway, intense solar radiation bakes brick veneer and lap siding. Heat transfers straight through empty wall cavities into your living areas. According to the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), air leakage and uninsulated exterior walls account for up to 40% of total residential energy loss. Professional wall injection foam seals wall voids around electrical outlets and plumbing, reducing summer AC strain and maintaining stable room temperatures.

Non-Invasive Exterior Installation Process

Our team installs injection foam insulation strictly from your home's exterior elevation. We work cleanly around brick veneer, vinyl siding, wood lap, and Hardie board without damaging your indoor living space.

✓

1. Thermal Scan & Mapping

We use infrared thermal imaging cameras to inspect wall cavities, locating studs, wiring, and existing insulation voids prior to drilling.

✓

2. Exterior Access Drilling

Technicians drill small 5/8-inch access holes through mortar joints on brick homes or temporarily lift exterior siding strips.

✓

3. Low-Pressure Cavity Fill

Aminoplast liquid foam resin is injected under low pressure. It flows around electrical boxes and wiring, filling voids without pressure that could bow drywall.

✓

4. Custom Mortar & Siding Patching

Access ports are filled with custom-mixed mortar matching your existing brick elevation, or siding is securely re-attached.

R-Value, Soundproofing, and Fire Safety Performance

Engineered to meet International Residential Code (IRC 2021) insulation standards and EPA SNAP guidelines for indoor air quality.

R-18 to R-20 Thermal Resistance

Injection foam delivers R-4.6 to R-5.1 per inch. In standard 2x4 exterior wall framing, it creates an R-18 to R-20 barrier that out-performs degraded fiberglass batts.

80% Exterior Noise Reduction

Dense foam absorbs outdoor sound waves, boosting your wall Sound Transmission Class (STC) rating by 10 to 15 points. It dulls traffic noise from US-290, Grand Parkway, and local lawn equipment.

Pest Barrier & Class A Fire Rating

Non-organic materials eliminate food sources for termites, ants, and mice. The fully cured foam is non-flammable and will not ignite or feed a flame.

Zero-VOC & Odorless Curing

Water-based aminoplast foam releases no harsh chemicals or off-gassing. Your family stays safely inside your home throughout the entire installation.
