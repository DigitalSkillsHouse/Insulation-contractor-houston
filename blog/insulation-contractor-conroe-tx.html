Insulation Contractor Conroe TX — Local Guide to Attic & Spray Foam Services

Published October 19, 2026  ·  By Insulation Contractor Houston

Conroe TX falls in IECC Climate Zone 2A, which requires attic insulation of R-38 to R-49 per DOE guidelines. Most Conroe homes built before 2005 have under-performing insulation. A licensed insulation contractor serving Conroe TX should be able to inspect your current R-value, recommend the right product, and provide a written estimate before any work begins.

Why Conroe TX Homes Need Attic Insulation Upgrades

Conroe is the county seat of Montgomery County, located about 40 miles north of downtown Houston. Like the rest of the greater Houston metro, Conroe experiences long, hot summers with relatively mild winters. The primary job of attic insulation here is to block radiant and conductive heat from entering the home through the ceiling — not to retain warmth in winter.

Many homes in established Conroe neighborhoods — including those in and around Lake Conroe, Teas Crossing, and the older subdivisions near Loop 336 — were built with code-minimum insulation that has since settled or degraded. A home that started with 6 inches of blown-in insulation may now have 4 to 5 inches due to compaction, putting it at R-13 to R-16.

Upgrading to R-38 to R-49 typically reduces the heat load on an AC system enough to cut cooling costs by 15 to 30 percent over a summer, according to Department of Energy data on Climate Zone 2A retrofits.

Attic Insulation Options for Conroe TX Homes

Blown-In Fiberglass — Attic Floor Installation

Blown-in fiberglass is the most common upgrade product for Conroe TX residential attics. Loose-fill material is blown to a uniform target depth from R-38 to R-49 directly over the existing insulation or onto a clean attic floor after removal. It is the most cost-effective way to reach current code recommendations.

Cost: $1.00 to $2.50 per square foot installed.

Open-Cell Spray Foam — Roof Deck Encapsulation

Open-cell spray foam applied to the underside of the roof decking creates a fully conditioned attic space. Rather than insulating at the floor level, the entire attic becomes part of the conditioned envelope of the home. This eliminates duct heat gain — a significant source of cooling inefficiency in Conroe TX homes where HVAC ductwork runs through the attic.

Cost: $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot installed.

When to Consider Insulation Removal First

Old insulation should be removed before new material is installed when it shows moisture staining, pest contamination (rodent debris, nesting material), or mold growth. Adding new insulation over a contaminated layer seals the problem beneath it rather than resolving it. We assess the condition of existing material during the free inspection.

Cost: $1.00 to $2.00 per square foot for removal.

What to Look for in an Insulation Contractor in Conroe TX

When evaluating insulation contractors for your Conroe TX home, confirm these before booking:

  • Licensed and insured — ask for a certificate of insurance before any work begins
  • Written estimate — a detailed line-item quote before work starts, not a verbal ballpark
  • R-value targets specified — the quote should state the installed R-value you are paying for, not just the product
  • Air sealing included — sealing penetrations before insulation is installed significantly improves the effective R-value
  • Removal vs. top-off clearly explained — a contractor who recommends removal should be able to explain why your current material warrants it
